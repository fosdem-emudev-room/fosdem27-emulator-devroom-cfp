# [FOSDEM 27 - Emulator Development Room CFP](https://fosdem.org/2027/schedule/track/emulator/)

Due to popular demand, we have decided to revive the Emulator Development Room, and the Call for papers is hereby open!

Once again, FOSDEM 27 will take place at
the [Université libre de Bruxelles, Belgium](https://fosdem.org/2027/practical/transportation/), on **30 & 31 January
2027**.

### What we're looking for

We want talks that dig deep. Instead of many short talks, we host fewer, longer sessions so speakers have time for real
technical detail, demos and discussion. Show us how things actually work under the hood.

Some ideas (for inspiration):

- Console and computer emulator development
- CPU emulation, binary translation, JIT and dynarec
- Graphics, audio and timing accuracy
- Test ROMs, conformance suites and measuring accuracy
- Reverse engineering undocumented hardware
- Debuggers, tracing and tooling for emulator developers
- Hardware on hardware emulation (FPGA and friends)
- OS emulation and virtual machine development
- Preservation, emulation history and keeping projects alive
- Running an open source emulator project: community, forks, handovers and legal pressure
- ...

#### Talk formats

- **Deep dive:** 30 to 50 minutes, including Q&A. This is the heart of the room.
- **Lightning talk:** 5 minutes. Perfect for a first emulator, a neat trick or a work in progress. First-time speakers,
  this one's for you!

#### Topic overlap

Some topics might be suitable or overlap with other rooms (such as
the [Retro](https://fosdem.org/2027/schedule/track/retrocomputing/) & [Virtualization](https://fosdem.org/2027/schedule/track/virtualization-and-cloud-infrastructure/)
rooms). In such cases we might contact the speaker and point that out, but ultimately it is up to the speaker where the
talk should land.

#### Desirable speakers

Experienced speakers are very welcome, but hobbyists and/or first time speakers are equally welcome. As long as you have
passion and/or knowledge you care to share. Never given a talk before? Submit a lightning talk, or reach out and we'll
gladly help you shape your proposal.

### How to submit your proposal

Submit your talk through [pretalx](https://pretalx.fosdem.org/fosdem-2027) and select the **Emulator Development**
track.

### What should be in your submission

- name
- short bio
- contact info
- title (funny titles are ~~required~~/appreciated)
- format: deep dive or lightning talk
- abstract: what you're gonna talk about, and what the audience will learn
- level of technical depth, and whether you'll do a live demo

### Important Dates

- **November 23rd: submission deadline** (this one is firm, as FOSDEM needs our full schedule by early December)
- December 1st: selected speakers notified
- December 7th: schedule published
- January 30th & 31st: FOSDEM!
